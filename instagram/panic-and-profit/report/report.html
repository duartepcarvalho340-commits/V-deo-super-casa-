CEO out. Record quarter.

Publicado

FormatoReel
ÁreaEarnings beyond the headline
Instagramhttps://www.instagram.com/reel/Df5AszXirgx/

Legenda (publicada em inglês)

CEO out. Record quarter. 5 days apart. 

Nov 12: Jensen Huang stepped down and CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. $NVDA fell 4.3% that day.
Nov 17: her first earnings. Revenue $115.8B (+103% y/y) vs ~$108.9B expected, and a $130B guide for next quarter.

The lesson: key-person risk is not the same as business risk. The numbers held. The open question is who leads next, and that's what to watch.

The Fed is about to hike. Again.

Agendado
DataQua 18 nov 2026 · 23h Lisboa · 18h Nova Iorque
FormatoReel
ÁreaFed, rates and US macro
Instagram—

Legenda (publicada em inglês)

The Fed is about to hike. Again. 

September brought the first rate hike since 2023. October's CPI came in hot (+0.4% m/m, core +0.3% vs 0.2% expected). Now markets price roughly 9-in-10 odds of another hike on Dec 9.

Why your stocks care: higher rates shrink what future profits are worth today. $100 ten years out is worth ~$74 at 3%, but only ~$61 at 5%. The further out the profits, the bigger the hit.
