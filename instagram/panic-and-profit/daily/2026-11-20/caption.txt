Same shopper. Three very different stock reactions.

Target: same-store sales +5.6% vs 3.8% expected. Best sales growth in 5 years. Stock up ~7%.
Walmart: beat and raised its outlook. Stock still slipped: its sales forecast fell short of what Wall Street wanted.
Home Depot: sales grew, but fewer customers came in and it cut its profit outlook. Stock down ~3%.

The lesson: stocks don't react to good or bad. They react to better or worse than expected.
