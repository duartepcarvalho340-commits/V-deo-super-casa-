BULL CASE vs. BEAR CASE

Cheap multiple, or peak earnings?

CHEAP
  • Hypergrowth at a value multiple: PEG ~0.1
  • Guide implies +12% q/q on Rubin ramp
  • Moat: CUDA + full-stack systems
  • Below-market P/E with ~75% margins
TRAP
  • Cyclical math: low P/E at peak E is the classic semis trap
  • GM guide down ~340 bps to 71-72%
  • Capex funded by thinning FCF; a 2027 cut hits E, not just P
  • Key-person risk + vendor financing of customers

The debate isn't the P. It's the durability of the E: is FY27 a plateau or a peak?

Sources: Nvidia guidance, Barclays, Fortune, CNBC, Yahoo Finance.
