WEDNESDAY'S BIG NUMBER

PCE, in plain English

PCE = how much prices rose on everything Americans spend money on. It's the Fed's favorite inflation gauge. Core PCE strips out food and energy, which jump around.

CORE PCE · SEPT
3.1%
year over year
OCT · EXPECTED
~3.2%
consensus, may change
FED TARGET
2%
long-run goal

Inflation is moving away from the target, not toward it. That's why the Fed is talking hikes, not cuts.

4/7
TWO SCENARIOS

Hot vs. cool

HOTTER THAN 3.2%
  • A Dec 9 hike looks locked in
  • Talk of a bigger-than-usual hike gets louder (a couple of Fed officials raised it in the minutes)
  • Higher rates press hardest on pricey growth stocks
COOLER THAN 3.2%
  • Gives the Fed room to slow down
  • Eases pressure on rates and valuations
  • Could calm a market running on fear

One number won't set the long-term path. But after a -8.6% week, it can set the mood.

Sources: October FOMC minutes (Federal Reserve), Nacionale, CME FedWatch.
