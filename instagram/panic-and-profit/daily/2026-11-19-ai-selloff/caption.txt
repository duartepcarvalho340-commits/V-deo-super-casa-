AMD fell as much as ~9% in one morning. The trigger: a short seller.

Hunterbrook, which disclosed a bet against the stock, published a report alleging that a Chinese server maker on the US Entity List since 2025 kept shipping updates with newer AMD firmware.

AMD denies it, saying the claims misrepresent its business and that it complies with US export law. The report itself does not claim proof of a violation.

By the close: AMD down ~7.7%, Nasdaq down ~2%.

The lesson: a short report is a claim, not a verdict. Watch for the company's answer, and for regulators.
