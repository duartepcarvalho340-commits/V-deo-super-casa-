Nvidia just staged one of the biggest comebacks in its history. 🚀

The stock jumped more than 25% this morning (up as much as ~34%), one of its best days ever.

Why? Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as president and CEO, effective immediately, just 11 days after he stepped down. President Trump had urged the board to bring him back.

The whole market followed: the Nasdaq opened more than 6% higher.

But remember: on Friday, Nvidia lost 14% in a single day. And the SEC review of its accounting, reported by Reuters, is still out there (no charges; the SEC declined to comment).

For long-term investors: the CEO is back. The questions are not gone.
