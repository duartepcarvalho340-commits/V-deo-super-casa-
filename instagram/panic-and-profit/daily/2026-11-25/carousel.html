PLAIN ENGLISH

What PCE actually measures

PCE = how much prices rose on everything Americans spend money on: rent, food, healthcare, cars, haircuts. It's the Fed's favorite inflation gauge.

Core strips out food and energy, which jump around. It shows the trend underneath.
